Our Story

Built in Philly

Founded in 2010 by former pro fighter Marcus "The Hammer" Williams, Iron Fist Boxing & MMA has been the training ground for Philadelphia's toughest fighters. We're not just a gym — we're a family.

Located in the heart of North Philadelphia, our facility has produced regional champions and helped thousands of everyday people transform their lives through the discipline of combat sports. We believe everyone has a fighter inside them.

Whether you're training for your first amateur bout, looking to get in the best shape of your life, or want to learn self-defense, Iron Fist is where champions are made.

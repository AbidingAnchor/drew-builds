Our Story

A Philly Tradition

Founded in 2015 by master barber James "Jimmy" Thompson, Sharp & Fresh has become a cornerstone of Philadelphia's grooming scene. We believe a great haircut is more than just a service — it's an experience.

Located in the vibrant Fishtown neighborhood, our shop combines classic barbershop traditions with modern techniques. Every chair tells a story, every conversation builds community, and every cut reflects our commitment to excellence.

Whether you're a lifelong local or just passing through, you'll leave looking sharp and feeling fresh. That's our promise to you.

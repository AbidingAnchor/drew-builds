About Noir

The Experience

Noir Lounge is Philadelphia's premier destination for those who seek sophistication, mystery, and unforgettable nights. Tucked away in the heart of Northeast Philadelphia, our speakeasy-inspired lounge offers an escape from the ordinary—a place where time slows down and the night truly comes alive.

Our expert mixologists craft signature cocktails that push the boundaries of traditional mixology, while our curated playlist sets the perfect mood for conversation and connection. Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or simply unwinding after a long day, Noir Lounge provides an atmosphere that is both intimate and electric.

From the moment you step through our doors, you'll understand why Noir Lounge has become the go-to spot for those who appreciate the finer things in life. Welcome to the night.

We believe church should feel like home.

Anchored Church is a welcoming community for people from all backgrounds — whether you're exploring faith for the first time or have been part of a church your whole life. We create space for real connection, honest conversation, and meaningful growth.

No perfect people allowed. Just a community committed to loving God, loving people, and making Philadelphia a better place together.

What We Believe

Four pillars that guide everything we do as a community.

Faith

We trust in God's love and grace as the foundation of everything. Faith isn't about having all the answers — it's about taking the next step.

Community

Life is better together. We build authentic relationships where people are known, valued, and supported through every season.

Purpose

Everyone has a calling. We help people discover their gifts and use them to make a real difference in our city and beyond.

Grace

We lead with grace, not judgment. Everyone belongs here — no matter where you've been or what you're going through.
